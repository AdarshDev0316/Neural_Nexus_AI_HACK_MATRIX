**🧬 Gamified Educational Platform for Advanced Pharmaceutical Sciences (PSHT-19)**


**📌 Overview**

This project presents a **gamified educational platform** designed to simplify and enhance learning in **pharmaceutical sciences and clinical research**. It transforms complex theoretical concepts into **interactive simulations and gameplay-driven experiences**, improving understanding, engagement, and practical decision-making.


**🎯 Problem Statement**

Core pharmaceutical concepts such as **molecular interactions, drug design, chemical kinetics, and clinical trial planning** are often abstract and difficult to visualize using traditional teaching methods. This results in low engagement and limited practical understanding among students.


**💡 Proposed Solution**

We propose a **HealthTech-driven EdTech platform** that integrates:

* Gamification techniques
* 3D molecular visualization
* Drug development and clinical trial simulations
* Adaptive assessments

This enables learners to **build molecules, simulate drug approval workflows, and make strategic decisions** in a risk-free virtual environment.


**⚙️ Core Features**

* **3D Molecular Builder**
  Interactive drag-and-drop assembly of atoms and functional groups for molecule design.

* **Drug Development Simulation**
  Scenario-based gameplay covering preclinical research, clinical trials, and approval stages.

* **Clinical Decision Challenges**
  Strategy-driven tasks that mimic real-world pharmaceutical decision-making.

* **Adaptive Quiz Engine**
  Personalized assessments based on learner performance and knowledge gaps.
